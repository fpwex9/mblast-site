About the company

A focused practice for complex private structures

Mindblast Limited is a private company incorporated in the Republic of Cyprus in 2026, with its registered office in Limassol. The company draws on almost two decades of experience in investment banking, prime brokerage, investment products and family office management.

We work with a small number of clients at a time: families with assets and entities in several jurisdictions, the family offices that serve them, and their holding companies.

Every engagement is agreed in writing and billed as a consulting fee. There are no products to sell and no commissions involved.

What we do

Services

01

Strategy & management consulting

Governance and operating model for family offices: decision-making and reporting processes, internal controls, organisation, and the selection and oversight of external providers.

02

Coordination services

A single point of coordination between the client and its banks, custodians, lawyers, tax advisers and auditors – onboarding and documentation, follow-up, and project management of structural changes.

03

Reporting & consolidation

One consistent view across institutions and jurisdictions: data collection, consolidated reporting and management dashboards for the family and its advisers.

04

Banking & investment infrastructure

Selection and set-up of banking, custody and brokerage relationships and of portfolio-reporting systems, based on hands-on experience from the industry side.

05

Special projects

Independent second opinions on service proposals from banks and other providers – terms, fees and operating model – together with provider selection and focused research.

Who we work with

Clients

  • Single-family and multi-family offices
  • Private clients and families with international assets
  • Holding companies and other private structures

Our clients are based mainly in Cyprus, Portugal and other EU countries, Switzerland and the United Kingdom. We work with professional and private clients only and do not offer services to the general public.

How we work

Approach

Independent

We sell no financial products and receive no commissions or retrocessions. Our only revenue is the consulting fee agreed with the client.

Confidential

Family matters stay private. Engagements run under strict confidentiality, with NDAs where required.

Practical

Deliverables are working processes, documents and systems that the family and its team actually use.

Please note: Mindblast is a consulting company, not a financial institution. We do not manage assets, hold client funds, receive or execute orders, or provide regulated investment advice. Client assets remain at all times in the client's own accounts, under the client's sole control.
